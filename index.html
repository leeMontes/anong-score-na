ABOUT ANONG SCORE NA?

How it works.

Scoring, what each page does, and where your data lives.

01 / SCORING

How scoring works

  • Games go to 11 points — the first side to reach 11 wins (no win-by-2).
  • When a side reaches 11 the game ends, and the higher score wins.
  • Doubles: the middle shows Server 1 / 2 — tap it to switch, and use the serving-side selector. Singles: one server (the number is hidden).
  • Add points: tap a team's score (+1), two-finger tap (−1), or use the +/− buttons. Undo and Reset sit below.
  • The timer starts when a match starts and stops when it ends.
02 / PAGES

What each page does

  • Scorecard — live score, serve, and timer, plus fullscreen, rotate, and a “Score card tester” for a quick untied card.
  • Players & matches — add players, auto-draft a round robin, play and finalize matches, see live standings, and “Wrap up day”.
  • Sessions — your archive of wrapped-up days, each with its schedule and standings.
  • All Time — your combined record across every session.
  • Info — this guide.
03 / DATA

Where your data is saved

  • Everything is stored in your browser's localStorage — no account, no server, no cloud.
  • Data lives only on this device and browser. Clearing site data erases it, and it won't sync to other devices.
